A note on guest counts

The guest maximum counts everyone in the building — children and adults together. A party of twelve children usually brings twenty to twenty-five adults with it, so pick your package with the grown-ups in mind.

Party questions

The things everyone asks

How far in advance should I book?
Weekend slots typically fill three to six weeks ahead, and faster in the fall and around school breaks. If you have a date in mind, book it early.
Can I book a party outside of weekends?
Yes. Weekday evening slots run 5:30 – 7:30 pm, and Monday holidays open up the full weekend schedule.
Is the space private during my party?
Completely. The play center is yours alone for the duration of your party, plus setup and breakdown time.
Can we bring our own cake and food?
Absolutely, and most families do. Bring the cake, the pizza, whatever you like. We provide the tables and, in packages 2 and 3, the themed tableware.
What happens to the deposit if we cancel?
Deposits are non-refundable — they hold a slot we then turn away other bookings for. If you need to move your date, reach out as early as you can and we'll do what we can to reschedule.
Who cleans up?
We do. Every package includes full staff cleanup. You leave with the gifts and nothing else.
Do adults count toward the guest maximum?
Yes. The guest maximum is everyone in the building — children and adults together, up to 49 for our largest package. Plan your list accordingly.
