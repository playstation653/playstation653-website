The member hour

Members get the first hour of the day on weekday mornings before we open to walk-ins — a quieter room, fewer bodies, and a much easier time for babies and new walkers.

Party discounts stack

Conductor members take 10% off any birthday package. On the Grand Central package that's $75 back — more than a month of membership.

Membership questions

Before you sign up

How does a membership pay for itself?
At $12 a visit, a monthly membership breaks even after the fourth visit. If you're here weekly, you're saving roughly half.
Can a membership be shared between siblings?
The Conductor covers one child. Punch passes are transferable between siblings.
Can I cancel a monthly membership?
Yes, any time, effective at the end of your current billing month.

Questions before you commit?

Call and ask. Memberships are month to month, so the worst case is you try one for thirty days.
