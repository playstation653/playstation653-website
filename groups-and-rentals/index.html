For teachers & directors

A field trip that fits in a morning

You're ten minutes off Route 136 with free parking directly out front of the side entrance on Ridgeway Drive and no bus-loading problem.

Bring your special group here to play together and make new friends along the way. There are many Play Stations to choose from, which enables and encourages group play. Keep the whole space to your ratio. Bring eight or more children and receive a discounted rate of $10 per child, and we let teachers and required adults in for free.
